Our Story

Pav Mantra started with one mission — real Mumbai flavor, no shortcuts.

Every masala is ground in-house, every pav is baked fresh on-site, and every plate is built the way it's done on the streets of Mumbai — quick, honest, and packed with flavor. It's a small, self-service kind of place, but the taste travels far.

  • In-house ground masalas, no shortcuts
  • Freshly baked pav, daily
  • Quick service, pocket-friendly pricing
  • Dine-in, takeaway & home delivery
